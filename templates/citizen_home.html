ℹ️ Critical Instructions During Disaster Operations (आपदा के समय ध्यान देने योग्य बातें)

🌊 Flash Flood / Rising Waters

Turn off main electrical breaker. Move to the highest reinforced terrace or roof. Do not walk or drive through moving water. Signal rescue helicopters with bright cloth or flashlights.

⛰️ Landslides & Mudflows

Stay alert for sudden changes in water flow or rumbling sounds. If rocks or debris approach, run immediately to nearest high ground away from valleys and steep slopes.

🌋 Earthquake / Structural Failure

DROP, COVER, HOLD ON. Stay away from glass windows and power lines. If trapped under rubble, tap on pipes or walls; avoid shouting except when hearing rescuers to conserve oxygen.
